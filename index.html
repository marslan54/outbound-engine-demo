How it works

Apolloaccounts + people from the client's ICP
Clayemail waterfall, verify, company facts
Clauderesearch, score, write, call brief
QA gateno invented numbers, spam or bad links
GoHighLevelcontact, deal stage, call task
Instantly3-email sequence, own copy per lead

Stop-on-reply across channels: an email reply or a call conversation stops the Instantly sequence and tags the contact in GoHighLevel. Then Claude reads the reply and three rules decide the next step: meeting, pause 90 days, or do not contact, everywhere.

The QA gate

Before anything reaches Instantly, every email is checked: numbers must come from the client's proof or the account's research, email 1 must open with a researched detail, no spam phrases, no links the client did not allow, no fake "re:" or "as we discussed", under 130 words. Dashes are repaired and the sender and opt-out line are added. The database refuses to queue a contact whose copy did not pass.

In the acceptance tests a model that invents "300% growth" is blocked. Running real Claude output through it also found two false alarms (a timeframe like "6 to 12 months", and "free up your team"); the gate was fixed and both are now test cases.

Tested like software

Acceptance checks through the real n8n workflows

  • intake runs for one client
  • Apollo is searched with the client's ICP and an API key
  • every contact reaches an end state (Clay calls back, each step hands on)
  • one account per domain, one contact per person
  • Clay gets each contact once, with the callback and its secret
  • the Clay waterfall result is saved: verified email and which provider found it
  • a Clay callback with the wrong secret changes nothing
  • catch-all and invalid emails are not contacted
  • a competitor in the brief's exclude list scores tier C and is never written to
  • research is versioned and cites Clay facts
  • holdout accounts are scored for comparison but never contacted
  • every sent sequence has 3 emails, no dashes, a sign-off and an opt-out line
  • copy with an invented statistic is blocked by the QA gate and never reaches Instantly
  • tier A contacts get a call brief, tier B do not
  • one GoHighLevel contact and one opportunity per contacted person (no duplicates)
  • every GoHighLevel call carries the token and Version header
  • contacts carry tags and the tier and score custom fields
  • opportunities land in the stage for their tier
  • tier A contacts get a call task with the brief (opener, questions, objections)
  • each contact goes to the client's Instantly campaign with its own approved copy
  • running the search again adds nobody twice anywhere
  • an email reply stops the sequence: the lead leaves Instantly
  • an interested reply moves the deal to Meeting with a booking task and a Slack alert
  • a webhook delivered twice is recorded once
  • a webhook with the wrong secret is refused
  • a call conversation stops the email sequence too (stop-on-reply across channels)
  • 'not now' pauses the contact for 90 days and moves the deal to Not now
  • an unanswered call is not a reply
  • an unsubscribe goes on the do-not-contact list, tagged and closed in GoHighLevel
  • the database refuses to put a do-not-contact person back into a sequence
  • a hard bounce marks the email invalid
  • the morning report shows each client's funnel, holdout and QA blocks
  • a bounce rate above the limit raises an alert
  • a second client runs from its own brief into its own GoHighLevel location
  • when Instantly is down, contacts wait (not lost) and an alert goes to Slack
